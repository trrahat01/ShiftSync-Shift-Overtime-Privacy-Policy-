# Privacy Policy (template)

Last updated: 2026

**ShiftSync** ("we", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what
data the app collects and how it is used.

## 1. Data you create

The app stores your shift schedules, settings, and worker profiles (including
photos) **locally on your device**. This data is never sent to us.

If you choose to enable **Supabase sync**, your data is stored in your own
Supabase project that you configure — we do not operate or have access to that
database.

## 2. Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob (and may use Start.io) to display ads. Ad networks
may collect and process data such as:

- Device identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID / Ad ID)
- Approximate location
- App usage data

This data is used to deliver and measure advertising, and is subject to the
ad network's own privacy policy:

- Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Start.io: https://www.start.io/privacy

## 3. Camera & photos

The camera and photo gallery are used only to capture and store worker profile
photos and to read details from ID cards/rosters you choose. Photos are stored on
your device and are never uploaded unless you enable your own Supabase backend.

## 4. Permissions

- **Camera**: used to capture worker photos.
- **Photos**: used to import existing photos.
- **Internet**: used to load ads and (optionally) sync to your Supabase project.

## 5. Your choices

You can delete all app data at any time from **Settings → Clear all data**. You
can also opt out of personalized ads in your device's ad settings.

## 6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer before using the app.
